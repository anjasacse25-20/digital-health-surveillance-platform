# Digital Health Surveillance Platform 🏥

## 📖 About
This repository contains my first hackathon project, developed during the Internal Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025 at CMR Institute of Technology.

I participated during my college bridge course, even before regular classes had begun. At the time, I had little to no programming experience, but I wanted to challenge myself and understand how hackathons worked.

Working with my teammates, I learned the basics of ideation, collaboration, rapid prototyping, and presenting a solution under time constraints. Looking back, this project marks the beginning of my software development journey.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
A Digital Health Surveillance Platform designed to improve health monitoring and disease reporting through a digital solution.

- Figma
- AI-assisted prototyping
- Team Collaboration
